I am a Research Analyst at the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, where I work in the Microeconomics Department.

My research interests lie in developing and using quasi-experimental methods to study questions of inequality and labor markets.

I graduated Phi Beta Kappa with the Highest Departmental Honors from Wesleyan University in 2026 with a B.A. in Economics, Mathematics, and Data Science.
